Sometimes I think about how random it was that we met — just a small party at res, back when we were both still figuring out university and life. I don't think either of us knew then that we'd end up making so many memories together.

Looking back, I think that's one of the things I love most about us. It wasn't always about doing something special. Sometimes the special part was simply having you there.

So today, I hope you know how grateful I am for every laugh, every random conversation, every meal, and every little moment that somehow became part of our story.
